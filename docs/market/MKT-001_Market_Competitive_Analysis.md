## 3. Competitive Moats & Defensibility

2. **Compound Style Memory (Persona Vault)**: High switching costs once an enterprise's tone, vocabulary, and visual assets are indexed and tuned.
3. **C2PA Cryptographic Provenance**: Pre-built compliance and authenticity verification demanded by enterprise legal and regulatory departments.
