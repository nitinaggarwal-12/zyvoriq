## 1. North Star Metric (NSM)
> **High-Confidence Assets Published (HCAP)**: The weekly count of multimodal assets published across workspaces with $\text{VQS} \ge 90$ and zero manual rollbacks.

### Supporting Diagnostic Metrics:
1. **Creation Velocity (Time-to-Validated-Impact)**: Target $< 45\text{min}$.
2. **Veritas First-Pass Yield**: Target $\ge 88\%$.
3. **Workspace 7-Day Publish Retention**: Target $\ge 65\%$.
